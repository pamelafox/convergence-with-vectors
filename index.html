Let's play Convergence

  1. Everyone stands in a circle.
  2. Two people stand in the center facing each other.
  3. Everyone counts down, "3..2..1"
  4. The two people both yell a word at the same time, like "fire" and "whale".
  5. They return to the circle, and everyone thinks: "What single word is at the intersection of those two words?"
  6. Two new people enter the circle and yell their words together, like "blubber" and "engine".
  7. If they say the same word, everyone wins! If not, the game continues.
  8. Play multiple rounds until you've had your fill.
