Play Convergence with embeddings

Pick two starting words and two "player" models. Each model independently applies the chosen operator to the current pair; their outputs become the next round's pair. The round stops when both models agree, a pair repeats, no candidates remain, or the round limit is hit. Words must come from the fixed ~1000-word vocabulary, since that's the only vocabulary with precomputed embeddings for this page.
