Play Convergence with embeddings

Pick two starting words and two "player" models. Each model independently applies the chosen operator to the current pair; their outputs become the next round's pair. The game stops when both models agree, a pair repeats, no candidates remain, or the round limit is hit. Words must come from the fixed ~1000-word vocabulary, since that's the only vocabulary with precomputed embeddings for this page.

Scoring formula√(max(a, 0) × max(b, 0))

Here a and b are a candidate's cosine similarities to the two input words, not the two models. With normalized embeddings, centroid and mean have equivalent rankings for a fixed pair (apart from numerical rounding near ties), but different score scales. If the input vectors cancel exactly, centroid scores are all zero.

Each model chooses the highest-scoring eligible word from the fixed vocabulary; there is no model inference during play. Current input words are excluded, but older words may return. Scores are rounded to nine decimal places for ranking, with alphabetical tie-breaking. Similarities and operator scores are not probabilities or confidence estimates.
